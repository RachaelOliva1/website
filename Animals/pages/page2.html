Silly Cats

Cats are naturally mischievous and silly because their wild instincts collide with a safe, comfortable domestic life. In a home environment, their finely tuned predatory drive—which includes stalking, pouncing, and sprinting—has no real outlet, so it transforms into the "zoomies" or sudden midnight antics. Their intense curiosity drives them to explore every high shelf, squeeze into impossibly tight boxes, and knock objects off counters just to see how they fall. Furthermore, cats quickly learn that acting goofy or causing a little chaos is a highly effective way to get their owner's undivided attention, turning their playful misbehavior into a rewarding game.
